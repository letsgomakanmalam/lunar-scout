- Claude (Anthropic), November 2026, sixth round: removed the colour mismatch at the top of the Live Earth map. The ground is now the same Blue Marble everywhere, and where no geostationary satellite sees, clouds are taken from the daily picture and drawn like the live ones.

Reading NASA's palette files showed both infrared palettes need decoding (the VIIRS one is warm = bright; the GOES/Himawari one is an enhanced colour scale), and that the earlier brightness rule would have drawn the coldest storm tops as holes; both are now decoded to temperature (src/lib/irPalettes.ts, checked against 31 palette entries).
